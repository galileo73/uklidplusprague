# Logo Instructions for Business Plan Presentation

Please add the UKLID PLUS PRAHA logo to the business plan presentation.

Files:
- Presentation: `docs/business-plan/UKLID_PLUS_PRAHA_Business_Plan_Strategy.pptx`
- Logo: `docs/business-plan/logo.png`

Instructions:
1. Add the logo to all 3 slides.
2. Place it in the top-right corner or bottom-right corner, depending on layout.
3. Keep the logo small and clean.
4. Do not cover any text, charts, or important content.
5. Use the same logo size and position on all slides.
6. Keep enough margin from the slide borders.
7. Do not stretch or distort the logo.
8. If the logo background is transparent, keep it transparent.
9. If the slide already has a visual header, use the bottom-right corner instead.
10. Export an updated PPTX and, if possible, also a PDF version.
